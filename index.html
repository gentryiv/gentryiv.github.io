About Me

To steal a phrase from an MIT PCB design OpenCourseWare course I did: I love the art and science of engineering.
In my mind, the science of engineering is how you build something - the math and physics you harness to make something that gets the job. The art of engineering is why you build it - it's the satisfaction of making something that's elegant, and intuitive, and cool.
I chose EE because I love both... and because I'm a big DIY guy and I wanted to be able to do more stuff. With that, I've really enjoyed working on embedded systems: taking projects from idea to prototype, seeing them in action, and then improving them. Through those projects, I've done a good bit of system architecture, PCB design, sensor fusion, and edge compute - and I'm always looking to build on those and learn new skills/applications.
I want to work on things that makes the world a better place. That's pretty intentionally open-ended, but I'll add that I've been particularly interested in bionics, robotics, and wearables.
I'm a big fan of DIYing, interesting thought experiments, good conversations, and over-optimizing my life. You'll see a little bit more of what I like to do below.
If you got this far, thanks for reading. Hopefully I made enough grammar mistakes to convince you this isn't AI.
